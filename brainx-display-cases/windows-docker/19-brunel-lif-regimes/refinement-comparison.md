## Why changed scientific labels are not refinement gains

The prompt delegates unspecified scientific choices anew to each isolated agent. Baseline uses seeds 11/29/47; new run uses 101/202/303. Initial voltage range changes from [0,20) to [10,20) mV, synchrony definition from 1-ms F1/c1/P to 5-ms S plus stationarity, irregular CV boundary from 0.8 to 0.7, required complete ISIs from 10 to 20, peak band from 1–1000 to 1–500 Hz, and fixed raster window from first to last 500 ms. Both specifications were locked before their own simulation. No post-outcome tuning occurred, but the two neural protocols/assessment definitions are not identical.

Consequently, fast-SI changing from inconclusive to supported cannot be credited to the skill edit; the CV cutoff itself differs. Slow-case eligibility changing from ~98–99% to ~8–13% is strongly affected by the >=20-ISI requirement and finite 3-s window; it is not evidence of degraded biology. The first population peak changing from 667 to 333 Hz is not proof of a new oscillator: the later search excludes 667 Hz, and population peaks/harmonics are distinct from single-neuron rate. Do not infer causal parameter effects without a separate predeclared intervention.

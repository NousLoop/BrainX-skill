# Model derivation and scope

The voltage reference was delegated; take the resting voltage as 0 mV. Without arrivals, the linear membrane equation is dV/dt=-V/(20 ms), whose exact solution over a free interval h is V(t+h)=V(t) exp[-h/(20 ms)]. A delta current is specified by its voltage jump, so no arbitrary capacitance, resistance or finite synaptic filter is needed. Add 0.1 mV for each excitatory or external event and -0.1g mV for each inhibitory event. At a threshold crossing V>=20 mV, stamp a spike, reset to 10 mV and clamp for 2 ms. The endpoint at refractory expiry can receive jumps; the preceding interval was clamped, so no leak is accrued during that interval. Discard input during the clamp, as declared before the run.

For each postsynaptic neuron, an excitatory source is sampled 1000 times without replacement from its allowed E sources and an inhibitory source 250 times from its allowed I sources. Excluding self edges is a delegated graph convention. The matrices are pre-by-post, but exact degrees are enforced by target. A presynaptic spike emitted at endpoint t is delivered at t+1.5 ms, verified by single E and I impulses through the production delay and DeltaProj path.

The supplied per-source frequency is eta*20 mV / [(0.1 mV)*1000*(20 ms)]=eta*10 Hz. The superposition of 1000 independent Poisson processes is Poisson with summed rate 10000 eta Hz per neuron, independently across postsynaptic neurons. Thus at h=0.1 ms the count mean and variance are both eta, and at h=0.05 ms they are eta/2. Draw actual counts, including counts greater than one. This is an exact superposition identity on the grid; all within-bin arrivals are applied at the endpoint, which is a numerical approximation checked by halving h.

The mean external drift is JE*Cext*nu_ext=eta*theta/tau_m, so its deterministic equilibrium voltage is eta*theta. Above threshold drive, the deterministic external-only free flight from reset to threshold is tau_m*ln[(eta*theta-reset)/(eta*theta-threshold)], plus the refractory duration. This checks the single-neuron implementation, but says nothing by itself about network synchrony. The recurrent mean voltage drift adds JE*(CE*rE-g*CI*rI); its fluctuations and delayed feedback require explicit network simulation. No remembered or external network protocol is used to predict or enforce the labels.

Regime names require operational definitions beyond the supplied equations. The locked thresholds require both count dispersion at least five times the independent Poisson benchmark and at least 15% relative population modulation, plus a tenfold peak-to-median spectrum for synchrony. CV<=0.5 and CV>=0.8 separate substantially regular from substantially irregular spiking and deliberately leave an uncertain band. The supplied membrane clock 1/tau_m=50 Hz separates slow from fast. These are delegated conventions, frozen in advance, not universal or literature-derived boundaries. All continuous measurements, eligibility fractions and ambiguous outcomes are retained. A peak in any finite noisy spectrum is always reported, even when the oscillation predicate is not met; it must not be misrepresented as a verified rhythm.

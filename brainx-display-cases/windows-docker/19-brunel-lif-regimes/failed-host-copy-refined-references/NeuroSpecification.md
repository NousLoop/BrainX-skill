## Researcher request
- Question: do the four supplied parameter pairs support synchronous regular (SR), fast synchronous irregular (fast SI), asynchronous irregular (AI), and slow synchronous irregular (slow SI), respectively?
- Experiment: forward-simulation of a current-based, delta-jump point-neuron network, derived solely from supplied data and first principles. No papers, scientific examples, existing implementations, or remembered protocols may inform the design; this restriction applies to independent review too. Skills and public API contracts may be read for library mechanics only.
- Fixed data: NE=10000, NI=2500; every target has CE=1000 distinct E and CI=250 distinct I inputs, Cext=1000; tau_m=20 ms; absolute refractory=2 ms; threshold=20 mV, reset=10 mV; JE=0.1 mV, JI=-g*JE; recurrent delay=1.5 ms; conditions (g,eta)=(3,2),(6,4),(5,2),(4.5,0.9). Each external source fires independently at nu_ext=eta*theta/(JE*CE*tau_m). Here nu_ext=10*eta Hz per afferent, so per-neuron superposed drive is 10000*eta events/s. Do not replace shot noise by a constant current or a Bernoulli process.
- Required output: PNG of four conditions, each with global rate in 0.1-ms bins, dashed mean, and a fixed random 50-neuron raster beneath; PNGs and raw data for E/I rates, ISI CV and global-rate spectrum; dominant frequency, quantitative tests, independent Codex verdict, runnable code and raw evidence.
- Delegated choices (now fixed): seeds [101,202,303]. Each seed controls a separate graph, initial state and Poisson stream; reuse that seed's graph/initial state and random-number keys across conditions for matched comparisons. Display seed 101 and the final 500 ms, with one sample of 50 unique neurons drawn uniformly from all 12500 using seed 20261108 and fixed across runs.
- Each run lasts 4000 ms; exclude first 1000 ms from every scientific summary. This removes 50 membrane time constants and leaves 3 s for 0.333-Hz Fourier spacing. Finite-window limitations remain explicit. No equilibration-dependent extensions or selected favorable seeds.
- Voltage reference: zero leak equilibrium; between jumps dV/dt=-V/tau_m. With no supplied resting potential, choose the zero defining the supplied threshold/reset voltage differences. No lower voltage clamp, noise current, adaptation or synaptic filtering.
- Graph: uniform fixed indegree, without replacement within each source population; exclude autapses; no parallel recurrent edges. Independent choices for different targets. E and I projections share a common 1.5-ms delay. Connectivity stays fixed during each run. External afferents are independent across targets and recurrent activity, so exactly superpose their Poisson processes into one Poisson count per target per step.
- Initial condition: independent uniform V in [10,20) mV, no refractory neurons, empty recurrent delay history. This spreads phases without presupposing synchrony.
- Integration: dt=0.1 ms, exact exponential leak between grid endpoints, instantaneous recurrent and external jumps at endpoints, then threshold detection and reset. Spikes emitted at t arrive at t+1.5 ms. During refractory, hold V at reset and discard incoming jumps; a neuron is eligible at t_last+2 ms. At most one spike per neuron per step. Record event times at endpoints. Units remain physical in the model; dimensionless arrays are allowed at connectivity, random-count, observation, serialization and host-analysis boundaries.

## Inspected data contract
- Sources: task text only; workspace initially empty. No measured recordings or literature. Parameters are supplied modeling assumptions, and expected labels are hypotheses.
- Raw results: per run, all-neuron binary spikes on the 0.1-ms grid (losslessly packed if useful), per-bin E/I spike counts, final voltage, graph identity, configuration, code hash, environment/device identity, seeds and execution logs. Preserve all unfavorable runs too.
- Derived: post-burn E/I/all rates in Hz/neuron; individual ISI CV=population-standard-deviation(ISI)/mean(ISI), requiring at least 20 complete post-burn ISIs per neuron; report eligibility fraction and E/I medians/distributions. No CV inference from the raster subset.
- Spectrum: global population rate in 0.1-ms bins, mean removed; Welch density with 1000-ms Hann segments, 50% overlap. Dominant frequency is the maximum PSD bin in [1,500] Hz, reported even if no oscillation is supported. Also preserve raw full-window Fourier density. For peak concentration use total Welch power in [1,500] Hz and power within +/-2 Hz of the dominant bin. Peak-to-background is peak PSD divided by median PSD in [1,500] Hz, excluding +/-5 Hz around that peak.
- Rate/raster mapping: same exact physical window and neuron identities across conditions. Global dashed mean is the mean over the displayed window; full analysis means reported separately.

## Acceptance boundary
- Frozen synchrony statistic: bin global counts in 5 ms. Compute S=sqrt(max(Var(K)-Mean(K),0))/Mean(K), using population variance. This removes the Poisson counting floor and measures macroscopic fractional rate modulation. Synchrony supported if S>=0.10; asynchrony supported if S<=0.05; between these is inconclusive. The two cutoffs distinguish substantial (10%) from small (5%) modulation rather than claiming an exact phase boundary. Half-window S must fall in the same category as full-window S, otherwise stationarity is inconclusive.
- Regular supported if the median individual ISI CV<=0.50; irregular supported if median CV>=0.70. Require at least 50% of neurons eligible for either conclusion. Intermediate CV or insufficient eligibility is inconclusive. Report E/I CV too.
- Oscillation supported if peak/background>=5 AND peak concentration>=0.10. Fast SI requires synchrony, irregularity, oscillation, and dominant frequency>=50 Hz; slow SI requires those same predicates with dominant frequency<50 Hz. The 50-Hz division corresponds to one membrane time constant (1/tau_m), derived from supplied dynamics. SR requires synchrony and regularity; AI requires asynchrony and irregularity. Every condition must satisfy its requested predicates independently for all three seeds to support the requested regime. Mixed-seed evidence is inconclusive; consistent contradictory categorical evidence is unsupported. Do not force labels or retune tests.
- Stationarity: compare E/I rates in the first and second 1.5 s of analysis; if either differs by >20% of the full-window rate, flag transient/nonstationary evidence and withhold regime support. Explicitly report finite-duration and finite-network limits.
- Component checks before production: exponential leak; threshold/reset; exact refractory release; recurrent delay impulse; voltage sign and jump size; Poisson multiple events, sample mean/variance and cross-target independence; full graph fixed indegree/no-autapse/no-duplicate/sign contract; deterministic repeat; compare sparse event propagation with a small explicit sum. Accelerated compiled result must match the component/reference path.
- Numerical control fixed now: rerun all four conditions at dt=0.05 ms for seed 101, with the same physical durations, graph and initial state. Aggregate to 0.1 ms for outputs. Treat control as numerical sensitivity, not an extra evaluation seed. Same distribution of independent Poisson drive, not identical paths. Flag a material discrepancy if E or I mean rate changes by >10% OR supported regularity/synchrony/oscillation category changes OR dominant frequency shifts by >max(5 Hz,10%) when both spectra support oscillations. A flagged discrepancy makes that condition's grid-independent regime claim inconclusive; preserve baseline results and explain it.

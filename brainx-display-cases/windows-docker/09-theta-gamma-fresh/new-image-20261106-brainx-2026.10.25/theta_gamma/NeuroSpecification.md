## Acceptance boundary
- Evidence required for success, failure, or an inconclusive result: Independently verify gamma in the unmodulated baseline and each matched control. Primary nesting metric R=|mean(A_gamma exp(i phi_theta))|/mean(A_gamma); also 18-bin normalized amplitude profiles and KL modulation index. Illustrative nesting at the prespecified display case requires gamma-band RMS>0.005, R_mod−R_control>0.05, and R_mod above the 95th percentile of 200 independent within-cycle rotations of the gamma envelope. Require all three initial/observation-seed lanes to meet it for full support; otherwise report partial/refuted/inconclusive with values. The surrogate is a descriptive phase-alignment check, not population inference or mechanism identification. Retain low-amplitude or nonoscillatory regimes with an explicit validity mask, never force a nested label. No monotonic depth/gain effect is assumed; save all sampled values, including failures.
- Required baselines and controls: m=0 at every gain and lane, same initial State, same observation-noise sample per lane, same analysis window. Positive metric control known sinusoidal amplitude modulation, constant-amplitude null. Within-cycle rotation null rather than a global phase shift (which preserves PAC magnitude). Validate E-only proxy sensitivity, latent E gamma envelope, and baseline gamma. Theta-only plus the same noise checks direct input/observation leakage. Numerical convergence dt=0.05 ms at all gains for m=0,0.6,1.0, lane 0; compare frequencies (<2 Hz), gamma RMS (<5% where >0.005), and R (absolute difference <0.03). No significance inferred from deterministic initial-State lanes.
- Invalid-result conditions: Nonfinite output, out-of-domain population activity, bad shapes/time alignment or State reset, solver nonconvergence at tested conditions, broken metric controls, or failed paired intervention contract. A failed scientific expectation alone is a valid negative result.
- Allowed claims and explicit non-claims: Conditional sufficiency of externally modulated E/I feedback to create phase–amplitude nesting within sampled regimes, if observed. Report gain/depth effects only at sampled points. No biological truth, measured EEG, endogenous theta, causal mediation, unique mechanism, or universal parameter-region claim.
- Model/comparison rationale and decisive assumption checks: m changes only the E input; gain changes the recurrent loop. Compare m=0 at each gain rather than pooling controls. Independent external theta can be visible in EEG without gamma nesting; check theta-only leakage and latent envelope. Compare observation mappings and numerical resolution before interpreting PAC.
